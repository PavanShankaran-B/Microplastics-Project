HAZARDOUS EFFECTS OF MICROPLASTICS

LDPE
  • Sources: Grocery bags, squeezable bottles, packaging films.
  • Possible effects:
    • Can stay in gut and liver.
    • Causes mild cell stress and inflammation.
    • Some additives may affect hormones.
PP
  • Sources: Food containers, packaging, textiles, disposable products.
  • Possible effects:
    • Can build up in liver, kidneys, and gut.
    • Causes cell stress and may harm your immune system.
    • May affect energy production and fat metabolism.
    • Could cause mild inflammation.
PS
  • Sources: Cups, plates, packaging, cosmetics.
  • Possible effects:
    • Tiny plastic pieces can enter cells and damage DNA.
    • May produce harmful molecules (ROS) that stress cells.
    • Can cause inflammation and affect hormones.
    • Might disturb energy and fat metabolism.
PT
  • Sources: Plastic bags, bottles, packaging films.
  • Possible effects:
    • Can reach gut and bloodstream.
    • Causes oxidative stress, harming cells.
    • May block nutrient absorption.
    • Can trigger inflammation.
HDPE
  • Sources: Milk jugs, rigid containers, bottles, pipes.
  • Possible effects:
    • Can get into bloodstream and organs.
    • May stress cells and trigger inflammation.
    • Can affect energy and fat metabolism.
PET
  • Sources: Water bottles, soda bottles, synthetic fibers.
  • Possible effects:
    • Can enter gut and blood.
    • May affect hormones and metabolism.
    • Can stress cells and trigger inflammation.
